Historic City Map Maker

Historic City Map Maker makes a historical map of any city: old photos, vanished places, stories and maps of the past over today’s map, with an admin that does almost everything for you.

  • Old photos placed where they were taken, each with the direction the camera faced, beside places and stories.
  • Historical imagery (declassified USGS frames, Landsat, scanned maps of your own) found, georeferenced and published as layers.
  • An admin that sets up Google Cloud, Mapbox and USGS with buttons, in your own project, in Romanian and English.

Start your map

Sign in with Google and name your place: the map and its admin are installed in a Google project of your own.

Prefer the terminal?

With Node.js and the Firebase and Google Cloud command lines installed, run:

npx create-historic-city@1.0.0-beta.1 self-host
