## 2. What works end to end on the emulators

Each line is proved by tests that drive the built admin and the built viewer from outside (Playwright, with the network
firewall, the console and placeholder guards, and every state through the generic layout and accessibility checkers at
the admin's five projects), by the functions' handler tests over the ports' fakes, by the rules tests, and by the
pipeline's pytest suite. `e2e/scenarios.json` names the test of every row.

- __Find__ (A11): the first visit, then once a month, harvests the three declassified USGS datasets over the city
  (`imagery.search`, 300 s) and ranks the scenes 0–100, free first, each with its reasons in plain words; the filters
  narrow the list and the map's footprints together; a browse image is copied into our bucket server-side (the browser
  never asks USGS); zero results say what to try; maintenance, a silent USGS and a refused token each say what to do
  (Try again, the link to Setup); before USGS approves downloads, the results show and Import waits with why.
- __Import__ (A12, A17, A18): one click on a free scene's card (the input budget counts 1); the dispatcher starts one
  Cloud Run execution per job; the version page follows the steps live, with the log and Cancel (the run's SIGTERM, the
  scene free again); a failed run is retried from its failed step; USGS maintenance waits, then resumes on the tick;
  too few matching points wait for the georeferencer. Under the city's 15 m threshold the policy publishes it with no
  other click; over it, the approval card shows both errors and the compute units, and Approve publishes. The fake MTS
  publishes, the manifest is rebuilt, and the built viewer paints the layer and fades it with its opacity slider.
- __The real pipeline__ (P01, P02): `python -m hcm_pipeline run` from the quality image's pipeline venv (the image the
  Cloud Run Job ships on) imports the 1966 frame from the admin, against the Firestore and Storage emulators and the
  mock USGS, STAC and Overpass answering a synthetic scene; the pytest suite covers every step, the resume after
  SIGTERM, a duplicate execution, a corrupt archive, an expired signed address, a size mismatch, no reference, uploads
  with and without a georeference, re-georeferencing and crop.
- __Upload__ (A14): a GeoTIFF through the Storage SDK's resumable upload (refused in the page when it is not a TIFF,
  resumed after a dropped chunk) becomes its own job; its own georeference goes to Approve, a plain scan to the
  georeferencer; a replacement for a layer asks no year and keeps the layer's tiles until the new version is published.
- __The georeferencer__ (A16): the scan and the reference side by side, pairs by pointer or keyboard, the residual
  table, both errors, the transforms each pair count allows, the warped preview; pairs on a line refused; Save continues
  the waiting job or makes a new version of a published layer; Crop on a published layer renders it again inside the
  polygon, and the viewer's tiles end there.
- __Publishing and the guard__ (A42): auto-publish off makes every version wait; a publish over the month's compute
  units waits with three ways out (also when the policy approved it), the free Studio path ending with the tileset the
  admin uploaded as the layer's source and `studioUploads` counted; nothing reaches Mapbox without a way chosen.
- __The budget pause__ (A43, CR-M3-47): when the month's Google Cloud cost reaches the budget, `ops.onBudget` turns
  `ops/guard` red with the alert's spend; Imagery and Jobs say so with the cost and the budget, an Import is refused
  with why, and __Resume processing__ (asked first, `imagery.resumeGuard`) turns the guard green for the rest of that
  budget period. The public map never stops.
- __What the two reviews fixed__ (CR-M3-47): a publish reaches Mapbox once (a start with an MTS job polls it, the same
  file is not uploaded twice, a lost answer is found in the tileset's jobs) and starts at most 6 times; a red guard holds
  a publish before Mapbox and Cancel ends one that has not reached it; a layer's deletion keeps its id taken and never
  deletes a newer version's files; the tileset cap counts first publishes under way, queued deletions and the month's
  new-tileset reservations in the guard's transaction; the two-runs cap is a document read and written in the dispatch
  transaction; a harvest that wrote nothing backs off (an hour, doubled) instead of running every five minutes; Cancel,
  Reject, re-georeference and Replace check their preconditions in their transactions; "I uploaded it" never takes the
  layer's own tileset; the address guard's timeout bounds the whole download. On the screens: every failure names its
  way out (Setup's Mapbox step, Upload another file, the free Studio upload for a file too large, Dismiss), Try again
  only where a retry can cure, "Needs you" counts versions the guard stopped, USGS maintenance offers Try again and one
  outage no longer blocks the area, a frame not scanned stops offering Import, the georeferencer places pair after pair
  by keyboard and on a phone, and the ranking's reasons and the plural forms read right in both languages.
- __Link a layer__ (A15): Check asks one tile server-side through the address guard and says what answered; broken
  sources keep Link off.
- __Polish__: the admin's tab title and `<html lang>` follow its language (an English browser gets "Map administration").

## 3. What waits for you (the real cloud)

Each step is yours (accounts, consents, payments, secrets) or a deploy that needs your yes. Claude prepares the commands
and opens the exact pages; nothing is done without you.

1. __Blaze and provisioning__: the project is on Blaze with the 5 USD budget (M2); run the Setup Assistant's
   __Provision cloud__ again so the M3 items are applied: the private bucket's lifecycle for `work/`, `reference/`,
   `uploads/rasters/`, `usgs-browse/` and the rasters (`PRIVATE_BUCKET_LIFECYCLE`), the APIs `run` and
   `artifactregistry`, and the IAM of §9.4 (`job-raster@` reads the two USGS secrets only; `fn-events@` may run the
   pipeline's job; for now on the whole project, `DECISIONS-for-review.md` §47d).
2. __Deploys__ (ask first, each one): the functions (`firebase deploy --only functions`: 16 new `imagery-*` functions, 51 in all,
   the `imagery-publish` task queue, `imagery-tick` every 5 minutes), the Firestore rules and indexes and the Storage
   rules, the admin (Hosting), the pipeline's image (`pipeline/Dockerfile`, target `runtime`, pushed to Artifact
   Registry) and the two Cloud Run Job definitions (`raster-pipeline`, 4 CPU / 16 GB, and `raster-pipeline-xl`,
   8 CPU / 32 GB, running as `job-raster@`).
3. __USGS M2M__: the M2M access request is approved by USGS (24–48 business hours; the app checks every 6 hours and turns
   the card green), and the application token is pasted in Setup's USGS card (60 seconds).
4. __Mapbox secret token__: pasted once in Setup's Mapbox card with the tileset scopes (the publisher reads it from Secret
   Manager; the pipeline holds none).
5. __Bucharest's target years__ (CR-M3-28): add 1966, 1976, 1977, 1978 and 1980 once in Settings → Historical imagery
   (the bundled seed does not carry them; changing `seed/bucharest/` is ask-first).
6. __The opt-in `real-data` run__ (ask first, local only, never in CI): import one free Bucharest frame end to end and
   report its checkpoint RMSE. It also settles what §14 of the contract marks __UNVERIFIED__: the M2M metadata field
   names, whether USGS honours HTTP Range and redirects, the MTS recipe and its price per km² (calibrate
   `KM2_PER_COMPUTE_UNIT`), Mapbox Studio's tileset page, Earth Search's `visual` asset, Overpass's limits, and how the
   corner guess behaves on a real Corona frame.
