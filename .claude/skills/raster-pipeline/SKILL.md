# Raster Pipeline Skill

Working notes: `.agent/skills/pipeline/raster_pipeline_skill.md`, `.agent/skills/pipeline/usgs_m2m_skill.md`, `.agent/skills/pipeline/mapbox_publishing_skill.md`. Status (M3, 2026-10-24): every step of `python -m hcm_pipeline run` is built and tested against loopback fakes and synthetic scenes, and ran once under the admin e2e tier against the emulators (`S-P01-HAPPY-ADMIN`); the contract and the as-built notes are `docs/research/M3-interfaces.md` §5 and CR-M3-19; what waits for the real cloud: `docs/research/M3-status.md`.

## Commands

```bash
PIPELINE_STAGE=tests bash scripts/ci/run-all-checks.sh   # the test stage; the pipeline's pytest gate runs pipeline/tests
python3 scripts/ci/pipeline.py --list                    # which gates exist today
# one synthetic scene with a known transform (delivery .tgz, Sentinel-2 COG, Overpass lines, truth.json), in the image:
docker run --rm -u "$(id -u):$(id -g)" -e HOME=/tmp/h -v "$PWD:$PWD" -w "$PWD" hcm-quality:local \
  bash -c 'PYTHONPATH=pipeline/src /opt/hcm/pipeline-venv/bin/python pipeline/tests/synthetic/scenes.py --out reports/scene --seed 7'
# the real pipeline under the admin e2e tier (the emulators, the mock USGS, STAC and Overpass):
docker run --rm -u "$(id -u):$(id -g)" -e HOME=/tmp/h -e E2E_TIER=admin -v "$PWD:$PWD" -w "$PWD" hcm-quality:local \
  bash -c 'mkdir -p $HOME && npm run build:site:e2e && cd e2e && npx playwright test admin/specs/imagery/pipeline.spec.ts --project admin-l1440-ro'
```

## Must know

- `osgeo.gdal` comes from `ghcr.io/osgeo/gdal:ubuntu-small-3.13.3` pinned by digest; Python 3.14, so `requires-python = ">=3.14,<3.15"`; no rasterio wheels, no pip GDAL; the venv uses `--system-site-packages`.
- Steps are short, idempotent and resumable: `fetch`, `extract`, `stitch`, `inspect`, `locate`, `reference`, `georef`, `warp`, `export`, `pyramid`, `register` (`JOB_STEPS` of `@hcm/shared`), run in order by `python -m hcm_pipeline run`. The task exits while it waits on USGS or the admin's points; `imagery.tick` resumes it. Publishing to MTS is not the pipeline's: functions publish after approval (the pipeline holds no Mapbox secret).
- M8 (contract: `docs/research/M8-join-interfaces.md`, as built §13): the job `layer-join`, queued only by `imagery.join`, runs `align` and `merge` (`steps/`, `join/`), then `export` and `register` unchanged. It reads each frame's `scan.tif` and resamples once; a correction a second measure finds unhelpful is refused, and absolute error is not improved. The thresholds are calibrated on synthetic scenes only, until rehearsal D3; `scenes.py --join` writes two frames side by side, and the real path runs in `S-P05-HAPPY-ADMIN`. A warp runs on GDAL's reverse transform, so a node is a place and the pixel `reverse` reads there; a thin-plate part keeps its own points as nodes; test a transform through a real warp on a non-affine frame (`docs/LESSONS.md` 366). Since M15 every georeferenced scan warps with `-tps` through `steps/warp.py` `rendered_points` (an affine or poly2 fit sampled on 17 x 17 nodes, a KH-9 pan camera's stored `georef.lattice`), and the join's base transform is the same one: the layer is the fitted map (`docs/research/M15-calibration-accuracy.md`, *As built*; `docs/LESSONS.md` 460). Of two KH-9 cameras mirrored about the nadir that hold the same tiles the plainest is kept (`camera.py` `plainest`, lesson 462); the georeferencer shows a version's stored figures and previews its lattice until its pairs change (461).
- What it writes (job, step records, layer versions, log lines) must equal the golden documents of `pipeline/tests/contracts/`, which the shared TypeScript schemas also validate; its paths equal `paths.json`, its transforms reproduce `georef-cases.json`, its constants equal `enums.json`.
- The georeferencing reference is OSM plus Sentinel-2. **Mapbox Satellite is never used to align imagery.** No `fostulbucuresti` resource is ever used.
- A proved frame is lined up with the accepted proved frames it overlaps, held still (M15 *Joint solve*, `calibrate/joint.py`, `joining.py`, `ties.py`, `steps/aligning.py`, `docs/LESSONS.md` 465): film ties on each one's `align.tif` (5 ground metres, kept by `export`), nine times a tile's weight; a partner over 6 m from the rest is left out, the furthest first; nothing published moves, and a frame of the admin's points is never held (no chain). A KH-9 pan frame keeps its camera; any other takes a cubic restricted to its film's measured box. The local worker reads the city's versions and their `align.tif`, read-only (M7 §2.5). One partner that cannot be read, looked up, staged or measured is left out with one job-log line naming it, and the frame fits with the others or alone; a lost lease still stops the run. Both object stores raise the network's errors as `ObjectStoreError` and `FirestoreStore` Firestore's as `DocumentStoreError`, so a caller catches the port's own error (`docs/LESSONS.md` 481, 482, 483).
- A KH-9 pan frame over relief its camera shows is fitted over the city's Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (M15 *Terrain*, `calibrate/terrain.py`, `surface.py`, `steps/relief.py`, `georef/rpc.py`, `georef/heights.py`): rows fitted where the film shows them (height times the view's tan), the map back to the ground through the heights (300 m cells, to the decimetre). The record carries its RPC and the heights under its scan, so every reader shows the layer's map: the warp, a join's part and the next frame's ties through `steps/warp.py` `attached` (`RPC_DEM` over the record's heights), the georeferencer through `terrainTransform`; the lattice samples the ground map (no lattice holds it: never render it for such a frame). Under 1 m of relief beyond a second-order fit once scaled by what its tiles show, without a camera or without every surface tile readable, the frame is fitted flat exactly as before, with a warning. The layer carries the DEM's notice. Lessons 466 to 469: list every reader of a map and prove each shows the published one; refuse a partial surface whole.
- No Sentinel-2 date moves a city silently (M15 *The reference guard*, `agreement.py`, `docs/LESSONS.md` 464): some dates sit rigidly 10 to 13 m off the others, so the reference is the least cloudy of up to 5 covering sets (30 days apart) that agrees with the median of the others within 7.5 m; `reference.s2Check` records every set, its offset and the verdict. A vote of 5 survives 2 bad sets, not 3; a guard on a shared reference is proved against forced bad dates, since the film ruler cannot see a shift all frames share.
- MTS publishes (functions' `imagery.publish`) only after approval (or auto below the city's RMSE threshold, default 15 m) and within the compute-unit guard; over budget the admin chooses the free Studio upload, next month, or paying.
- Tests: the synthetic world and scan harness with a known transform, held-out checkpoint RMSE, hypothesis in a derandomised profile, the fake M2M server on loopback, deterministic threads and seeds; 90 % per file, lines and branches each (a `for` that can end without its `return` is a branch: test the exhausted loop).
- M9 (`docs/research/M9-calibration.md`): `locate` finds and proves a frame with no landmark (`calibrate/`: votes, dense tiles, proof, pairs), for any place. Never a cloud model: a local matcher only behind `calibrate/ports.py`, and its output is only a proposal. A gate over survivors says nothing of what left, so `calibrate/rival.py` looks once more with the search open; ranges are ground metres, never a share of the window; a place may lie over longitude 180 or across two orbit tracks (`docs/LESSONS.md` 374 to 377). Rehearse on real frames before claiming a figure (the tools and the table: M9 §14), and when a real frame refuses, look at its film before changing a gate: the 1982 frame's refusal was the model (a short film sweeps less and off the nadir), not the imagery. A film's aspect tells its sweep; its ends' widths tell where the nadir lies. The figure a frame reports must be held out **before** RANSAC, or it flatters. A run writes `calibration`, `georef.checkpointKind` and `output.coveragePct` (at most 100), all in the shared schemas: only `held-out` checks and, for a USGS frame, 90 % of the city publish on their own (`functions/src/imagery/approval.ts`), and most frames will wait for one click. `imageryPasses/{key}` is read here through `ctx.read`, written by `imagery.onJobWrite`.
- M16 (`docs/research/M16-recalibrate.md`): a recalibration is a `regeoref` version with `recalibration`; `steps/recalibrate.py`'s `RecalibratedGeorefStep` is the `georef` step for every job. Refine (`auto`) and Snap my points (`points`) rerun `measured_on_scan` and `calibrated` from the current fit (bent to the rough points), reading the city window's cached references since a `regeoref` has no `reference` step. Compare two fits **on the same checks**, the new fit's disabled pairs (`figure_on`), never each one's own figure: a dozen checks move a metre with the seed. A refine that renders nothing raises `DeclinedError` (`notImproved`, `refineRefused`): job and version end `cancelled` with the reason, never `failed`, so the layer is released and the current version stays. The transform the current version rendered with is refitted from its pairs: reconcile with the accuracy branch's camera model (M16 §9).
- M10 (`docs/research/M10-local-ai.md`): the local AI, a sidecar with no network. **Every failure ends at the admin's points, never a failed job** (`LocalMatcher.propose` lets only `RunStoppedError` through), and the run must leave `wanted` false however it ends; the starter's loop must never block on the sidecar (`docs/LESSONS.md` 383). The Windows starter may hold no `\"`, no empty argument to a native command and no quote inside one, for Windows PowerShell 5.1 (`docs/LESSONS.md` 382). A wide proof speaks for the window only through `Found.window_spread` (`docs/LESSONS.md` 385).
- Real KH-7, KH-9 mapping and KH-5 frames (M9 §14): a strip may lie as a parallelogram (`SearchRange.shears_deg`); a frame with no vote is read by eye and proved from a hand start before any search is widened; KH-5 refuses, honestly. No real Corona has run.
- As built (CR-M3-19): `locate` fetches the Sentinel-2 window (the votes and the proof read it) and `reference` reuses it, so a USGS import without a STAC item fails at `locate`; Firestore writes are compare-and-set on the read's update time (five tries), Cloud Storage is its JSON API over httpx (`google-cloud-storage` is unused); OSM is cached, drawn for the georeferencer and, since M4, a matching channel of `georef` (below); every client follows no redirect except the USGS delivery, each hop through the address guard.
- Under the emulators (`FIRESTORE_EMULATOR_HOST` or `STORAGE_EMULATOR_HOST` set) the pipeline is sealed: every service root and every address from data must be on this machine (`check_emulated_url`), and `EmulatorSecrets` answers the shared fakes' USGS user of the scenario (`e2e-ok` with `e2e-usgs-app-token-ok`); a fake that hands it an address hands a loopback one (`docs/LESSONS.md` 164, 165). Error parameters are texts (`{pairs}` as `"7"`).
- A step re-reads the job before it starts (a resumed run must not trust the launching run's copy), and the stitch template is narrower than the segments' overlap (`docs/LESSONS.md` 160).
- An import runs in two phases (finding 56, `docs/LESSONS.md` 328): the download (`DOWNLOAD_STEPS`) on the small `raster-fetch` job, which hands the job back `queued`, then the rest on `raster-pipeline` or `-xl`; an out-of-memory exit moves to the next larger job; a run whose job names no machine walks every step. A transfer reports its bytes with `ctx.report_bytes` (finding 55).
- What the pipeline writes is held to the shared schemas (finding 62, `docs/LESSONS.md` 332–333): `pipeline/tests/integration/test_written_documents.py` records every job, version and log document the real runner writes along ten paths and fails on a shape missing from `contracts/written-documents.json` (regenerate with `HCM_WRITE_CONTRACTS=1`, then Prettier; `pipeline-contract.test.ts` parses each one); a limit the contract declares (`MAX_SCAN_PIXELS`) is checked where the value is made; error params are texts (`StepFailedError` converts). The corner guess (finding 63, `docs/LESSONS.md` 334; lesson 361) scores only square-pixel assignments, a camera with a half angle through its panoramic model and on its exposure alone (`frame.json`'s `image_start` and `image_end`, either side of a turned scan); a low score is searched around by masked gradients, whose ratio and z can accept it. A guess nothing accepts is weak, no failure: the crop is the union of every orientation that reaches the window with a 10 km margin, never cut (over `MAX_SCAN_PIXELS` it fails and keeps the delivery), the delivery stays, and when the scan then waits for points its corner pairs go (`weakCorners`). `apiError.imagery.cityNotFound` is left for a frame that reaches the window under no orientation.
- No step holds a frame whole (finding 57, `docs/LESSONS.md` 329): segments are read by windows (`hcm_pipeline/frames.py`), `stitch` writes `frame.json` and `frame-preview.tif` with two segments staged at a time, `locate` reads the city's window from the covering segments; a test bounds the memory of a frame larger than the bound. A run killed without a word is noticed when its heartbeat is three beats late (finding 58).
- A delivery's size is not a Corona's (findings 83, 84, `docs/LESSONS.md` 360): a KH-9 panoramic frame has 14 segments and is about 500,000 px long. A limit fails the job, never cuts the input; and when a frame will not match, list the archive and render the frame before tuning a matcher. Test matching on images that repeat themselves, as snow-covered valleys do, not only on noise.
- USGS errors arrive with any status: JSON 500 `errorCode`, HTML 403 for a bad token, 503 HTML for maintenance (backoff, not failure). Signed URLs are redacted everywhere and the pipeline never runs in GitHub Actions.
- The budget alarm pauses the pipeline and MTS, never the site; an admin resumes it from the Imagery or Jobs banner (`imagery.resumeGuard`, for the budget period).
- M18 (`docs/research/M18-improvements.md` §3): `imagery.tick`'s `relocate` part sends a frame parked for points searching again by itself once the installed pipeline image differs from its `JobDoc.searchedWith` (three a city a tick); Locate again also takes a crop that failed at `stitch` or `locate`; a parked frame gets a run of its own, a failed crop's runs count (`attemptsExhausted` at `JOB_MAX_ATTEMPTS`); the day's limit counts only `usgs-import`, `geotiff-import` and `layer-join`. The tick compares only the image the Cloud Run job runs (never the `setup/status` record), and leaves a frame alone while its job's `placingPoints` mark (the georeferencer's) is younger than `PLACING_POINTS_HOLD_MS`.
- The publisher never pays twice (CR-M3-47, `docs/LESSONS.md` 171–174): a start with an MTS job polls it, the upload is skipped when the same file went up, the publish call is marked in a transaction before it (a retry lists the tileset's jobs), every start counts `publishAttempts` (at most `PUBLISH_MAX_ATTEMPTS`), the tick's recovery id is `stale-<job's last write>`; a 413 goes back to the admin with only the Studio way; the tileset cap counts in-flight first publishes and pending deletions. A queued `layer-delete` keeps its layer id until it runs.
- The tiles' alpha loses the film's dark rim and its fade, never the crop (`tiles/rim.py`, `docs/research/M12-own-tiles.md` *The dark rim*, lessons 497, 499 and 500): measure a rim from the alpha's edge as a Euclidean distance in ground metres against the scene's own level, not a fixed grey, grow its fade only where the profile still rises (a flat dark lake touching the rim is the picture's), and never feed the view a VRT pixel function (a derived band hides every overview: write the derived alpha once with the source's overview levels).
- M20 (`docs/research/M20-import-local.md`, as built §14): a local archive of scanned maps is read **where it lies**, by the owner's worker (`hcm_pipeline/archive/`, `worker/imports.py`): paths only through `archive/safepath.py`, a zip only through `archive/zipread.py`'s limits, a format only by its magic bytes, every decode in the spawned probe child (never `subprocess`, never forked: `docs/LESSONS.md` 520). Only series and counts go up (`worker.archiveReport`), never a host path or a per-file row. The job `archive-series` runs `gather` (every sheet's SHA-256 checked, `sheets.json` local) and `place` (a placer per class in `PLACERS`, else the version waits `needs_gcps` with `help` and the job `awaiting_gcps`, never `failed`), then the shared tail; its tiles follow `params.encoding` (`tiles/encoding.py`: line art lossless with its paper transparent, never JPEG). A rights record the report writes must pass the server's `rightsSchema` (a permission names who granted it: lesson 518). The placers (`hcm_pipeline/placing/`, §15) read sidecar world files themselves (lesson 522), fit pixels to Mercator with the flip (lesson 523), choose between coarse placements by the fine fit's agreeing pairs with a rival at most half as many (lesson 524), and find junctions by crossing number (lesson 525); a placement that does not hold waits with `help` and `junctions` suggestions. PDF pages come out through pikepdf only (never cracked). The owner's `hcm-archive.json` is read at the archive's root or in its one top folder; in the admin (§16), Upload → Import local files opens the worker's `/import` page, the report opens by itself and one Import makes the jobs; a new job type the pipeline runs joins every hand-kept list of them (lesson 529). The sidecar's entries merge from the root down, a deeper key overriding (lesson 530), and name a scan's original (`originalLabel`, `originalUrl`, `originalProbable`); the rights the server keeps are the report's, and only the city owner's confirmation changes them (lesson 531); an archive series' COG and Studio file never leave the computer (§17).
- A GDAL failure on the pipeline's own files is `apiError.imagery.processingFailed`; only reading an admin's upload (`inspect`) is `unreadableRaster` (`gdal_step(..., code)`).
- M4 (contract: `docs/research/M4-interfaces.md` §2, autogcp): `georef` adds an ORB fallback, OpenStreetMap's lines as a distance-field channel matched tile by tile, a confidence (`score`) and a residual per pair and held-out checkpoints (a fifth from 15 confident pairs); pairs from `AUTO_GCP_MIN_CONFIDENCE` (0.6) join the fit, lower ones are the version's `suggestions`, which only the admin's Accept and Save put into a georeference. Constants and golden shape: `pipeline/tests/contracts/autogcp.json`, `version.needs-gcps.suggestions.json` (`packages/shared/src/schemas/autogcp.ts`). As built (CR-M4-18): the OSM channel compares the white and black top-hat of the CLAHE'd pre-warped scan (lines up to 150 m wide) with OSM's proximity field, not Canny's edges (155 m off on the synthetic world, against 13 m: `docs/LESSONS.md` 194); a tile's margin is its peak minus the next local maximum outside the peak's 3 × 3 neighbourhood; OpenCV 5's `estimateAffine2D` returns no model for collinear points and NaNs for coincident ones, so both are checked. The e2e stand-in's outcome `suggestions` writes the golden shape (`e2e/admin/support/imagery-standin.ts`).
- M7 (contract: `docs/research/M7-local-worker.md`, DECISIONS §83): the same image runs on the owner's computer as `python -m hcm_pipeline worker --city <id> --project <id>` (package `hcm_pipeline/worker/`): a local page on `http://localhost:8765/` (Host, Origin, JSON and CSRF checks; the Firebase JS SDK from gstatic at the admin's version) signs the owner in, the tokens stay in memory, and a claimed job runs its steps unchanged with two ports swapped: `DocumentStore` over `worker.step`, `ObjectStore` routed (`usgs-raw/`, `work/`, `reference/` local; `rasters/`, `uploads/` through `worker.urls`' signed URLs); the M2M calls go through the server (`worker.urls` `kind: 'usgs'`), so the worker holds no USGS secret. `locate` deletes the job's `usgs-raw/` delivery once the crop and the version's `scan` are recorded (a retry downloads again; the bucket's lifecycle is 7 days).
- Findings 66–69 of 2026-11-02 (DECISIONS §85): `worker --pair <code>` (`worker/pairing.py`) signs in without a browser: `worker.pair` (no Authorization header), Identity Toolkit's `accounts:signInWithCustomToken` with the web API key and the Hosting origin as `Referer` (the key is referrer-restricted; the refresh sends it too), then `worker.hello` through `PageActions.sign_in`; the refresh token is kept in `/work/.hcm-session-<city>.json` (mode 600, the code's SHA-256 only) and resumed at the next start; a transient failure retries every 30 s; `RevokedError` (`apiError.worker.revoked`) ends the session and deletes the file; `outdated` from hello or claim is on the page. The image's runtime stage owns `/work` (the named volume `hcm-worker` copies it). The page's Google sign-in is a fallback that gives up after 90 s and shows the state without the SDK.
- Finding 72 of 2026-11-02 (DECISIONS §86): the starter file's supervisor runs `worker --supervised --image sha256:<digest>` (no `--pair`, no restart policy); a claim answered `outdated` then ends the loop and the command exits `ExitCode.OUTDATED` (75), so the supervisor pulls the installed image (`worker.image`). The local server answers CORS and Private Network Access's preflight for the project's Hosting origins only (`Endpoints.hosting_origins`): `GET /state` (now with `workerId` and `pairable`) and `POST /pair {code}`, which `PageActions.pair` hands to `Connector.offer` (paired at the loop's next pass, as `--pair`); any other origin 403 without CORS headers. Templates: `packages/admin/starter/` (shellcheck and shfmt lint the `.sh`; the `.bat` is structure-tested; since finding 73 the server's `worker.starter` serves them filled, `functions/test/worker/starter.test.ts`), and since finding 74 the supervisor says once "update the map" and waits 5 minutes after three usage errors (`2`) in a row: an image older than its `--supervised --image`.
- Signal handlers take no lock and lose nothing (`docs/LESSONS.md` 342, DECISIONS §83j): `RunFlags.interrupt` is one assignment; `sigterm_raises` also calls the Python handler it replaced (the worker loop's, never Python's Ctrl-C handler); the loop reads its stop flag every 0.25 s in its waits and a run reads it at every checkpoint (`RunSettings.asked_to_stop`). `pipeline/tests/integration/test_worker_stop.py` sends one signal to a worker process held at a moment (`pipeline/tests/fakes/stopping_worker.py`) and expects exit 0. A test that installs a handler puts it back: the autouse `signals_restored` in `pipeline/tests/conftest.py` fails one that leaks (`docs/LESSONS.md` 402).
